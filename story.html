From a Campus Initiative to a National Movement

Empowering Her to Own Her Future

What began as a simple vision to empower female students has evolved into a growing movement dedicated to helping young women recognize their beauty, value their intelligence, and build the balance needed to thrive in every area of life.

Join Movement Read Our Story
Chapter 01

The Seed

2022/2023 Academic Session

In the 2022/2023 academic session, Amanda Egbe, then President of APWEN ABUAD Collegiate, introduced the concept of Triple B.

The original vision was to create a platform where female students could learn, grow, acquire practical skills, and prepare themselves for life beyond the classroom. This marked the beginning of what would later become Triple B.

Chapter 02

The Expansion

2024/2025 Academic Session

During the 2024/2025 academic session, the vision was expanded under the leadership of Olonisakin Niniolaoluwa.

Triple B evolved beyond a single student initiative and became a platform designed to serve female students across every college and discipline. The focus shifted from a campus-based concept to a broader empowerment movement.

Chapter 03

The Launch

May 31, 2025

On May 31, 2025, Triple B hosted its inaugural event. More than 200 students participated in a day filled with inspiration, learning, community, and meaningful conversations.

Women from diverse industries shared their stories and experiences, helping students see new possibilities for their futures. The event proved that Triple B was more than an idea—it was a movement capable of creating real impact.

Chapter 04

Today

Active Movement

Today, Triple B is a registered NGO committed to empowering female students across tertiary institutions in Nigeria.

Through mentorship, leadership development, skills acquisition, partnerships, and community building, Triple B continues to create opportunities for young women to grow and thrive in their academic and personal lives.

Chapter 05

Tomorrow

The Future Vision

The vision extends far beyond a single institution. Triple B aims to build a national network of empowered young women connected through mentorship, education, leadership development, and opportunities.

The long-term goal is to become one of the leading empowerment platforms serving female students across Africa and beyond, inspiring generations to step into their potential.

Founder's Message

Olonisakin Niniolaoluwa

Founder & President, Triple B Global Initiative
“ You are not just joining a team. You are joining a movement. ”

When we set out to build Triple B, it was driven by a single realization: female tertiary students have immense potential, but they often navigate their academic and personal paths without structured guidance. We wanted to build a sanctuary—a community where a young woman doesn't have to choose between beauty, brains, or mental balance.

Every seminar, every digital workshop, and every mentorship session we host is designed to build this legacy. I invite you to join us, support us, and grow with us. Together, we are raising the next generation of Nigerian female leaders.

Our Mission

To empower female students with the skills, tools, opportunities, and mindset to own their future by recognizing their beauty, valuing their intelligence, and learning to balance every part of life.

Our Vision

To build a platform where female students across institutions are inspired to recognize their beauty, value their intelligence, and balance every aspect of life while unlocking their full potential.
